## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's the difference between residential and datacenter proxies for web scraping?**
Residential proxies use real IP addresses assigned by internet service providers to actual devices, making them harder to detect and block. Datacenter proxies come from servers in data centers—they're faster and cheaper but easier for websites to identify and block. Choose residential for target sites with strong anti-scraping measures, datacenter for high-speed general scraping where detection risk is lower.

**How many proxies do I need for my web scraping project?**
Start with 100-1,000 rotating residential IPs for most projects. If you're hitting rate limits or getting blocked, scale up to 10,000+ IPs or switch to unlimited rotating plans. For account-based tasks requiring persistent sessions, dedicated proxies work better than large rotating pools. Monitor your success rates and adjust—more IPs isn't always better if your scraping patterns look automated.

**Can I use free proxies for web scraping?**
Free proxies are unreliable, slow, and often compromised with malware or logging your data. Success rates rarely exceed 50%, and many targets block known free proxy IPs immediately. For any serious project, paid proxies deliver far better ROI through higher success rates, faster speeds, and actual support when things break. Even budget providers like IPRoyal or Geonode outperform free options by massive margins.
